About Me

Kathleen Salgado

Founder, Kat Consulting

My Mission & Vision

I came to education believing it could change lives and communities.

I wanted students to ask questions, think critically, understand the world around them and know they could shape it.

More than twenty years later, as a classroom teacher, reading specialist, coach and facilitator, that belief still guides me.

I’ve seen students accomplish far more than they thought possible when we give them tools, time and space to think deeply.

I’ve also learned that great instruction grows through collaboration. Some of my most successful years were built with teams who shared a vision and built on one another’s work.

I’m still learning. The teacher I was at the beginning isn’t the educator I am today and I hope that never stops.

Through Kat Consulting, I now work with schools and teachers to make effective instruction practical, meaningful, and manageable. We look at scaffolds, literacy, engagement, and planning in ways that fit real classrooms.

Because at the end of the day, I still believe education has the power to change the world and that change begins in our classrooms every day.

Credentials

Professional Background & Experience

My background includes teaching and instructional leadership across elementary and secondary education, with experience as a classroom teacher, reading specialist, instructional coach, professional development coordinator, and educational consultant.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Elementary Multicultural Education from the College of Santa Fe and a Master of Education in Curriculum and Instruction, with an emphasis in Reading and Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL), from Concordia University Portland. I also hold a New Mexico teaching license with endorsements in Reading and TESOL.

As a Master GLAD® Trainer, I’ve supported educators in developing language-rich, engaging instruction that makes rigorous learning accessible to all students. My work with Dual Language Education of New Mexico (DLeNM) has also given me opportunities to design, coordinate, and facilitate professional learning across New Mexico, nationally, and internationally.

My experience extends beyond any single instructional approach. I’ve worked with teachers, school leaders, and districts on literacy, academic language, scaffolding, student engagement, curriculum implementation, and sustainable instructional improvement.

Throughout that work, I’ve remained connected to classrooms and the educators who bring learning to life every day.
